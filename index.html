About

I am a third-year Computer Science undergraduate (Japan-Oriented Program) at Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology (HCMUT — VNU-HCM), pursuing research in artificial intelligence with a focus on computer vision, multimodal retrieval, and, more recently, dynamic 3D/4D scene reconstruction.

My research experience spans a multimodal video retrieval system, deep-mutual-learning-based image classification, hybrid filtering for real-time 3D motion tracking, and calibrated 4D Gaussian Splatting for multi-sensor scene reconstruction. I currently serve as Lab Manager at the HCMUT EE Machine Learning & IoT Lab and have worked as a teaching assistant for Python and Machine Learning courses.

I am committed to rigorous, reproducible research: reading and critically analyzing the literature, reimplementing key methods, and validating ideas through applied projects and competitions.

05 · Research & Projects

Projects

Four projects tracing a path from classical computer vision to multimodal retrieval and real-time dynamic scene reconstruction.

01

Multimodal Video Retrieval System Jun. 2025 – Dec. 2025

A multimodal video retrieval system: keyframe extraction with TransNetV2, semantic embeddings via BEiT-3/CLIP, object information via YOLO/Faster R-CNN, and OCR via PaddleOCR/EasyOCR/CRAFT. Fast retrieval with FAISS (cosine similarity) and MongoDB Atlas Fuzzy Search; query refinement with the Gemini LLM; multimodal and temporal querying algorithms for precise video moment localization.

TransNetV2CLIP / BEiT-3FAISSMongoDB AtlasGemini LLMOCR
02

Mushroom Images Classification Feb. 2025 – Apr. 2025

In-depth exploratory data analysis to extract statistical insights and class characteristics, guiding preprocessing and architecture choices. Trained ResNet, EfficientNet, and Vision Transformer (ViT) with Early Stopping, Automatic Mixed Precision, and learning-rate scheduling; combined multi-model predictions via Deep Mutual Learning — attaining approximately 96% accuracy on the private test set.

ResNet / EfficientNet / ViTDeep Mutual LearningAMP96% Accuracy
03

A Hybrid Filtering Approach for Enhanced 3D Motion Tracking Sep. 2025 – Dec. 2025 · Published, ICIC 2026

An adaptive hybrid filtering framework that dynamically combines low-pass, Kalman, and 1-Euro filters based on real-time motion-state classification (static, slow, fast). Integrates global trajectory estimation for physical plausibility, including ground-plane alignment, metric scale recovery, and footskate correction. Achieves a 30.9% reduction in jitter, minimal temporal lag (0.033 s), and real-time performance at approximately 293 FPS on complex martial-arts motion data.

Kalman Filter1-Euro FilterMarkerless Motion CaptureReal-time · 293 FPS
04

Photometric-consistent 4D Gaussian Splatting for Multimodal Sensor Alignment 2026 · Research Manuscript

Co-first author. Proposes the Geometric Confidence Score (GCS) — a per-pixel geometric confidence signal derived from temporally aggregated LiDAR data — enabling metric-scale 4D reconstruction of dynamic multi-person scenes from an asymmetric commodity rig of five static RGB cameras and one hand-held LiDAR phone. GCS down-weights dynamic regions in the photometric loss without any tracking or segmentation, and drives a confidence-weighted opacity regularizer that removes floaters. Outperforms 3DGS-Calib and image-only 4DGS baselines across all reported calibration, geometry, and appearance metrics.
